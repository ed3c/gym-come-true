# Issue plan — creator-market validation and launch evidence

## Outcome

Prove a retained-user acquisition loop with disclosed, rights-cleared creator content and honest attribution.

## Acceptance

- 30 problem interviews and seven-day concierge cohort.
- Twelve owned raw creative variants and creator scorecard.
- Contracts separate creation, posting, view make-good, raw footage, paid usage, territory, and term.
- Claims, sponsorship disclosure, music/font/person/property rights are reviewed.
- Cohorts track protocol activation, paid conversion, refund, day-30 retention, contribution, and safety complaints.
- Experiment ledger records stop/revise/repeat/scale decisions.

## Hard limits

- No fabricated comments, transformation, revenue, or reliability claims.
- No hidden sponsorship.
- No copied CPM treated as a forecast.
- No scale decision based only on views or installs.
