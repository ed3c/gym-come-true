# 90-day marketing plan and launch material

## Positioning

**Category:** evidence-first fitness protocol planner.

**One-line promise:**

> Scan what you actually use, confirm what the label actually says, and rebuild today's workout, meals, and reminders around the time you really train.

**Trust line:**

> Proof before advice. No automatic supplement dose. No scraped exercise GIFs.

The product should not use “biohack your body,” “optimize hormones,” “safe stack,” “medical-grade AI,” “guaranteed alarm,” or similar claims without a separately reviewed evidence and regulatory basis.

## Source benchmark warning

The request includes an Erly growth narrative, revenue/download figures, AlarmKit behavior, creator CPMs, and a 120-day timeline. Treat those as a user-provided benchmark that requires independent source verification before public use. This plan borrows the useful mechanics—native problem demonstration, tight scope, creator-native scenes, cohort economics—without repeating unverified figures as facts.

## Market entry story

Generic fitness ads show screens and feature lists. Gym Come True content should show a visible conflict:

```text
The clock changed.
The label changed.
The plan did not silently guess.
```

The app enters the scene as a tool already used by the creator, not as a five-second logo introduction.

## Audience wedges

| Wedge | Moment | Native creator scene | Product proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late-night lifter | work pushed training to 22:00 | commute home → dinner → gym → recovery → sleep | switch A to B; 00:15 remains next-day |
| Supplement organizer | several bottles and duplicated notes | morning counter with labels | scan candidates; manual confirmation; no dose advice |
| Privacy-conscious user | does not want label photos in the cloud | airplane mode demonstration | on-device extraction and temporary-file deletion |
| Evidence-focused coach | client plan changes every week | coach sends a versioned template | plan version and confirmation checkpoints |
| Indie fitness developer | cannot ship scraped GIFs | build-in-public screen recording | default-deny registry and original body view |

Do not target people through inferred medical conditions or sensitive health attributes.

## 90-day execution

### Days 1–15 — Problem proof

**Goal:** confirm that evidence and schedule switching solve a repeated problem.

Actions:

1. Recruit 30 interview participants from Taiwan fitness communities, existing contacts, and long-form content audiences.
2. Collect the last real protocol day, not hypothetical preferences.
3. Run a seven-day concierge test with the shared timeline and manual confirmation.
4. Build a waitlist page with three messages:
   - “16:00 or 22:00? Rebuild the whole day.”
   - “OCR is a draft. You confirm the label.”
   - “Your exercise media should have a license trail.”
5. Produce 12 raw vertical-video variants before buying distribution.
6. Define tracking events and guardrails before creator outreach.

Exit gate:

- at least 10 qualified users complete five verified protocol days;
- scan/entry confirmation is faster or more trusted than their current process;
- at least one message produces materially stronger waitlist intent;
- no safety or privacy defect requires hiding the real data flow.

### Days 16–30 — Closed alpha

**Goal:** prove first-session value and day-7 retention.

Actions:

- invite 50–100 users in controlled cohorts;
- test A/B plan switching and the late-night cross-midnight case;
- ask for notification permission only after the user creates a protocol;
- record OCR corrections without uploading raw images to general analytics;
- publish four founder/build-in-public posts showing evidence and rights decisions;
- recruit five small creators for unpaid product discovery, with no posting obligation;
- finalize sponsored-content disclosures and prohibited-claim list.

Exit gate:

- median first protocol under 10 minutes;
- no unconfirmed OCR value enters an executable plan;
- day-7 verified-protocol retention supports a paid test;
- creators can demonstrate the product in a normal routine without scripted medical claims.

### Days 31–60 — Creator-native launch

**Goal:** identify a repeatable content scene and paid-conversion cohort.

Actions:

- contract 8–12 creators across late-night gym, office life, student life, evidence-based training, and developer/build-in-public niches;
- require 2–4 concept variants per creator rather than one expensive polished ad;
- acquire reusable raw-footage and paid-usage rights separately from organic posting;
- use native disclosure and platform sponsorship tools;
- launch dedicated store/landing variants for each content concept;
- compare blended installs, activated protocols, paid conversion, refunds, and day-30 retention by cohort;
- repurpose only rights-cleared footage.

Budget is released in stages:

```text
concept sample -> approved native cut -> first post -> measured cohort
-> additional variants only when retention and payback qualify
```

Views alone do not qualify a creator cohort.

### Days 61–90 — Controlled scale

**Goal:** scale one or two retained-user loops without weakening trust.

Actions:

- renew only creators whose cohorts reach the payback and retention gate;
- turn the best scene into a repeatable creator template, not a copy-paste script;
- test annual versus monthly pricing and limited free tier;
- build referral around a shareable protocol template or completion receipt, not health claims;
- publish the first catalog provenance report and rights-clean asset release;
- begin coach-workspace interviews if consumer protocol retention is strong;
- pause channels that generate high installs but weak verified protocol days.

## Creator selection

Prioritize creators with:

- consistent recent views rather than one historical viral post;
- comments that show audience trust and routine questions;
- natural morning, workday, late-gym, meal-prep, or evidence-based content;
- willingness to show the product as one small part of a real scene;
- a clean history on misleading health and body claims;
- ability to deliver raw footage and usage-rights metadata;
- audience concentration in the intended language/market.

Follower count is a weak filter. Use a recent-post distribution, median views, comment quality, production reliability, and prior sponsor integration quality.

## Creator commercial protocol

A view-guarantee can control delivery risk, but it must not reward deceptive content. The agreement should separate:

1. creation fee;
2. organic posting deliverables;
3. expected or guaranteed aggregate views, with a reasonable make-good mechanism;
4. raw-footage delivery;
5. paid usage/whitelisting rights and term;
6. territory and platforms;
7. editing and derivative rights;
8. disclosure requirements;
9. prohibited health, medical, copyright, revenue, and reliability claims;
10. takedown, correction, brand-safety, and payment milestones.

Do not require fake comments, hidden sponsorship, fabricated results, or indefinite use rights buried in a view clause.

### Example commercial gate

```text
Initial test = small creation fee + 2 native posts + 3 raw hooks
Scale option = exercised only when:
  activated_protocol_cost <= target
  AND day_30_verified_protocol_retention >= target
  AND refund/complaint guardrails pass
  AND content rights evidence is complete
```

Use the creator's actual recent distribution to negotiate. A fixed CPM copied from another product or country is not a market fact.

## Attribution in a privacy-limited funnel

Do not force false precision. Maintain three views:

### Direct evidence

- campaign/store links where supported;
- creator-specific landing page or template code;
- redeemed referral code;
- survey response captured after activation, not before value.

### Cohort evidence

- post time versus install and activation lift;
- geography/language match;
- creator-specific onboarding choice;
- incrementality holdouts where volume allows;
- paid conversion and retention of exposed windows.

### Blended business evidence

```text
incremental contribution
  = incremental net revenue
  - creator and media-rights cost
  - store fees
  - variable service/model/media cost
  - support/refund reserve
```

If aggregate contribution and retention improve consistently, scale the cohort even when individual attribution remains incomplete. Document the uncertainty rather than relabeling it “organic.”

## Content principles

1. Show the conflict in the first second.
2. Use the app for no more than the time needed to resolve the conflict.
3. Return to the creator's normal story.
4. Let the audience ask about the tool; do not manufacture comments.
5. Demonstrate the safety stop as a feature, not a disclaimer hidden at the end.
6. Never show an unverified label amount becoming a recommended dose.
7. Never use a third-party exercise animation unless its campaign usage is in the media registry.
8. Display sponsored-content disclosure clearly.

## Ready-to-produce UGC concepts

### Concept 1 — “Two gym times, one life”

**Audience:** office worker or student with changing schedule.

**Opening frame (0–2s):** split-screen clocks: `16:00` and `22:00`.

**Traditional Chinese voiceover:**

> 我不是每天都能四點健身。以前時間一改，晚餐、訓練後補充、睡覺全部亂掉。

**Action (2–8s):** creator changes A to B; timeline moves and `+1d 00:15` remains at the end.

**Voiceover:**

> 我只換訓練時間，整天的流程跟著重排，而且半夜十二點不會跑到最前面。

**Trust beat (8–12s):** show `CONFIRMATION REQUIRED`.

> 補充品不會自己亂改劑量，標籤沒確認就停在這裡。

**Close (12–15s):** creator enters gym.

> 這才是我需要的健身行程表。

**English hook alternative:** “My workout moved to 10 PM. My whole day had to move with it.”

### Concept 2 — “OCR is not truth”

**Audience:** supplement organizer.

**Opening:** close-up of two similar bottles.

> 兩罐長得一樣，配方不一定一樣。

**Action:** scan one label; UI shows candidate count and evidence hash.

> 手機先讀文字，但它只是一份草稿。我還是要對照瓶身確認。

**Safety beat:** show IU/unknown-unit block in a prototype or static test fixture.

> 看不懂的單位，不是叫 AI 猜，是直接停下來。

**Close:** morning meal scene.

> 我想要的是可追蹤，不是更有自信的亂答。

### Concept 3 — “The midnight scheduling bug”

**Audience:** developers and quantified-self users.

**Opening:** timeline incorrectly places 00:15 before 22:00, red X.

> 很多行程表看到 00:15，就以為它是今天最早。

**Action:** show day offset and corrected order.

> 晚上十點訓練的恢復流程，其實跨到明天。這個 app 用 day offset 排，不靠字串排序。

**Close:** code-to-product transition.

> 健身 app 的 bug，有時候不是 UI，是時間模型。

### Concept 4 — “No scraped GIF dependency”

**Audience:** indie fitness developers and technical users.

**Opening:** broken remote GIF icon.

> 免費資料庫的 GIF 突然不見，你的 app 就只剩空白。

**Action:** show default-deny registry, empty media manifest, local body geometry.

> 我先用自己畫的肌肉示意圖。每個正式媒體要有授權範圍和 SHA-256 才能進產品。

**Close:** stable app screen.

> 少一點動作，也不要把整個產品蓋在盜鏈上。

### Concept 5 — “The app says stop”

**Audience:** trust-oriented users.

**Opening:** creator toggles “recent medication context.”

> 我最不需要的，是一個什麼都敢回答的 AI。

**Action:** show `BLOCK_AUTOMATION` and review reason.

> 有藥物、奇怪症狀、看不清楚的標籤，它不會幫我湊答案。

**Close:** creator closes screen and contacts a qualified professional in a neutral way.

> 會停下來，才是我願意長期用的工具。

Do not name a medication, imply a specific interaction, or stage an emergency.

### Concept 6 — “Morning protocol in airplane mode”

**Audience:** privacy-conscious users and travelers.

**Opening:** airplane mode turned on.

> 我的補充品標籤，不需要先上傳到雲端才看得到。

**Action:** local scan, temporary-image deletion message, timeline view.

> 先在手機上讀、確認、排進今天。模型掛掉，基本流程還是能用。

**Close:** commute/travel scene.

> 離線能做的，就不要假裝一定要 AI。

## Founder/build-in-public posts

### Post A — Rights ledger

**Headline:** “I deleted the exercise GIFs before I shipped the fitness app.”

Body structure:

1. show the attractive public dataset;
2. show the missing rights chain or hotlink risk;
3. show the `DENY/REVIEW/ALLOW` registry;
4. show the original schematic fallback;
5. ask developers which asset category blocks them most.

### Post B — Safety architecture

**Headline:** “The LLM is not allowed to decide the supplement plan.”

Body structure:

1. show OCR candidate;
2. show deterministic unit/context gate;
3. show immutable explanation flags;
4. show model output rejection example;
5. invite feedback on evidence UX, not dosing.

### Post C — Cross-midnight test

**Headline:** “A 22:00 workout broke my first schedule model.”

Body structure:

1. failing sort example;
2. `dayOffset` model;
3. regression test;
4. user-facing result;
5. ask for other real schedule edge cases.

## Landing-page copy

### Hero

**Traditional Chinese**

> 你的訓練時間變了，整天的計畫也該一起變。  
> 掃描真實標籤、自己確認、排出今天能執行的健身與飲食流程。

Primary CTA: `加入封閉測試`  
Secondary CTA: `查看資料與安全原則`

Trust strip:

> 裝置端辨識候選 · 不自動計算補充品劑量 · 無授權媒體不進產品

**English**

> Your workout time changed. Your whole protocol should change with it.  
> Capture real label evidence, confirm it yourself, and run the day you actually have.

### Three value cards

1. **不是把 OCR 當答案**  
   先讀取、再確認；看不清楚就停下來。

2. **16:00 / 22:00 一鍵切換**  
   餐食、訓練、恢復與跨午夜順序一起重排。

3. **每個動作素材都有來源**  
   不靠不明 GIF 或隱藏 CDN 維持體驗。

### Objection copy

**“它會建議我吃多少嗎？”**  
不會。它能整理使用者輸入與已確認標籤，顯示確定性和阻擋原因；個人劑量與藥物交互作用需要合格專業評估。

**“一定要上傳照片嗎？”**  
目前設計優先在裝置端擷取候選文字，暫存影像完成後刪除。未來任何雲端保存都必須另外同意。

**“鬧鐘一定會響嗎？”**  
目前是平台提醒，不宣稱系統級鬧鐘保證。精確鬧鐘與 AlarmKit 是獨立功能與權限階段。

## Store screenshot copy

## Creator brief

**Required scene:** a real moment where the workout time or label creates friction.

**Must show:**

- the creator's ordinary routine before the app;
- one product action resolving the friction;
- an evidence or confirmation state;
- clear sponsorship disclosure;
- only features present in the supplied build.

**Must not say:**

- “safe supplement stack,” “doctor-approved,” “medical-grade,” “guaranteed results”;
- “the AI tells you exactly what to take”;
- “the alarm cannot fail”;
- “all exercise media is copyright free”;
- unsupported income, download, conversion, body-transformation, or health claims.

**Delivery metadata:**

- original file and export;
- post URL and timestamp;
- platform analytics screenshots;
- music/font/stock/license declarations;
- people/property release status;
- allowed edit/platform/territory/term;
- disclosure screenshot;
- correction/takedown contact.

## Weekly content rhythm

| Day | Organic | Creator/paid | Product evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | A/B protocol story | late-gym creator | schedule regression test |
| Tuesday | label-confirmation tip | supplement organizer | OCR correction metric |
| Wednesday | build-in-public rights post | developer creator | registry delta |
| Thursday | routine template | office/student creator | activation and retention |
| Friday | “app says stop” trust story | evidence-based creator | safety receipt audit |
| Saturday | user routine remix | best cohort reuse | support/complaint review |
| Sunday | transparent weekly report | no forced post | next experiment decision |

Do not publish daily merely to satisfy a calendar. Stop content that attracts unsafe questions or misleading interpretations until the creative is corrected.

## Experiment ledger

Every content experiment records:

```text
experiment_id
hypothesis
creator + audience + market
hook and scene
build/version shown
claims checklist
rights/disclosure evidence
spend and committed deliverables
views and completion distribution
landing/store sessions
protocol activations
paid conversions and refunds
verified protocol retention
support/safety incidents
decision: stop / revise / repeat / scale
```

Keep the original hypothesis and negative result. Do not rewrite a failed campaign as “brand awareness.”

## Scale gate

Scale a content pattern only when all are true:

- the scene reliably communicates the problem in the first seconds;
- the cohort activates a protocol, not merely installs;
- retained contribution supports the acquisition cost;
- refund, complaint, and safety guardrails pass;
- sponsored disclosure is correct;
- every visual/audio/person/property right is recorded;
- the build shown matches the store build;
- the product team can support the resulting users.
