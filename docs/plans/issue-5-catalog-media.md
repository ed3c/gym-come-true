# Issue plan — copyright-clean catalog and licensed media pipeline

## Outcome

Ship a top-50 exercise catalog whose instructions, translations, muscle mappings, and media have reproducible per-record provenance and revocation behavior.

## Acceptance

- Canonical taxonomy and per-field provenance schema.
- Independent Traditional Chinese and English authoring workflow.
- Executed asset scope and private evidence references.
- Content-addressed originals and deterministic derivatives.
- Signed catalog/media manifests with build-time hash enforcement.
- Attribution, accessibility fallback, takedown, and kill-switch drills.

## Hard limits

- No scraped mirror import.
- No remote vendor CDN hotlink.
- No repository-level license treated as proof for every asset.
- No breadth target before top-50 retention evidence.
