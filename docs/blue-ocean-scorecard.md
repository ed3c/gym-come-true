# Blue-ocean validation scorecard

Use this scorecard during user research and competitor teardown. Scores must be linked to observed product behavior or source evidence, not memory.

| Axis | Weight | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Label evidence capture and correction | 10 | tested label flow and correction UX |
| Regional Traditional Chinese support | 9 | actual labels and locale behavior |
| Cross-midnight schedule correctness | 9 | edge-case tests and user flow |
| Privacy and offline usefulness | 9 | runtime network/storage evidence |
| Deterministic health-safety boundary | 10 | rules, tests, and decision receipts |
| Exercise/data provenance | 10 | per-record and per-asset evidence |
| Training-plan utility | 7 | completed real protocol days |
| Reminder reliability transparency | 7 | platform/device measurements |
| Coach collaboration | 5 | role and version behavior |
| Catalog/media quality | 5 | admitted top-50 coverage |
| LLM explanation controls | 7 | output schema and eval results |
| Price-to-retained-value | 8 | paid retention and contribution |

Score 0–5 per axis. Record uncertainty separately; do not award a score because a marketing page claims a feature.
