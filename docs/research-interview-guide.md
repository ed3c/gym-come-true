# Problem interview guide

Ask for the last real day, not opinions about a future app.

1. Show me how you planned your last training day.
2. What changed between the original plan and what you actually did?
3. Where are product labels, amounts, meals, and reminders stored now?
4. Tell me about the last time a training-time change broke the rest of the day.
5. Which field did you have to double-check on a physical label?
6. What information would you refuse to upload?
7. When should an app stop and ask for qualified review?
8. What happened after the last reminder failed or arrived at the wrong time?
9. Which exercise media do you trust, and why?
10. What would make you use a verified protocol for seven consecutive days?

Record artifacts, corrections, time, and observed friction. Do not provide supplement or medication advice during research.
